# ProfileSwap

Play WoW: Forever on more than one device? A PC with mouse and keyboard, a TV with a controller, a Steam Deck, or streamed to your phone. ProfileSwap saves each setup as a **profile** and switches everything at once.

## What a profile can switch

- **Display**: display mode and resolution (including a custom resolution)
- **Edit Mode layout**
- **UI scale**
- **Controller + Gamepad UI** on or off, and the **size of the Gamepad UI buttons**
- **AddOn set**: which addons are on
- **Addon profiles**: EllesmereUI, Naowh Forever and any AceDB addon (BigWigs, Questie, Zygor, ...)

## Also

- **Auto mode**: picks the right profile for your screen resolution at login.
- **On-screen picker** you can place anywhere through Edit Mode.
- **Controller friendly**: Esc > Options > AddOns > ProfileSwap has a Switch button per profile.
- **Smart reload prompt**: only when something really needs a reload, and it lists what changed.
- Neon and Plain skins.

## Getting started

Type **/pswap**, open **Profiles**, click **New Profile**. Full guide: [GitHub README](https://github.com/zakarulcodes/ProfileSwap#readme).
